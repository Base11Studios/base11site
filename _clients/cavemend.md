CaveMend is your home maintenance companion that helps you stay proactive about property upkeep. Never forget seasonal tasks or miss important maintenance again. The app guides you through a personalized setup, building a complete maintenance schedule based on your home type and location.

With CaveMend, you can document every task with costs, service providers, dates, and photos - creating a comprehensive maintenance history for your home. The app's season-aware scheduling automatically adapts maintenance timelines based on your geographic location, ensuring you're always prepared for what's coming.

Powered by Apple Intelligence, CaveMend makes adding tasks effortless with AI-powered task discovery through natural language input. Simply describe what you need to do, and CaveMend will help you create the perfect task.

## Key Features

- **Smart Setup**: Guided setup with expert task library customized to your home type and location
- **Complete Documentation**: Track costs, service providers, dates, and photos for every task
- **Season-Aware Scheduling**: Maintenance timelines that adapt based on your geographic location
- **AI-Powered Tasks**: Natural language task creation using Apple Intelligence
- **Customizable Reminders**: Push notifications to keep you on track
- **CaveMend Gold**: Manage multiple properties, share homes with family, view analytics and completion history, and unlock premium themes
