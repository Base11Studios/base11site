Moon Ritual is a quiet companion app for tending what matters, one moon at a time. Set an intention at the new moon, receive a daily message from the moon, and watch a personal altar fill with the artifacts of the cycles you have lived through. It is not a game, not astrology, and not a clinical tool. It is a calm, earthy place you visit to feel a little more integrated with the natural world.

Each day, Moon Ritual tells you what today's phase means for you, in plain second person, and then hands you one small thing to carry. There are no points, no levels, and no streak you can lose. The thing that accumulates is meaning and memory, not score.

Begin with the moon you were born under, set an intention you want to tend, and let the lunar cycle give the rhythm. Check in with a single breath, reflect by candlelight, and every full moon receive a letter that looks back on the moon you just kept.

## Key Features

- **A daily ritual:** open it tonight and the moon tells you what today's phase is for, with a short reading you can sit with.
- **Set an intention:** name what you want to tend and carry it across the whole cycle, from new moon to new moon.
- **Follow every phase:** a lunar calendar tracks the moon through its phases and names each full moon along the way.
- **One quiet check-in:** a single breath to notice how you are, really, without mood-disorder framing or therapy language.
- **Your birth moon:** begin with the moon you were born under for a reading that is personal from the first night.
- **A full-moon letter:** every full moon, a reflection that gathers the cycle you just lived and hands it back to you.
- **Keep an altar:** earn line-art artifacts that record the moons you have kept, a growing memory of your practice.
