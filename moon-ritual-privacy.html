Data Storage & Syncing

Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device for immediate access. This includes your intentions, check-ins, journal reflections, earned altar objects, and the moon you were born under.

iCloud Syncing (Optional)

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, Moon Ritual uses Apple's iCloud to securely back up and sync your intentions, check-ins, and journal entries across all your Apple devices. This syncing:

  • Is completely private to you and stored in your personal iCloud account
  • Is not accessible by Moon Ritual or Base11 Studios
  • Follows Apple's iCloud privacy and security standards
  • Can be disabled by signing out of iCloud on your device

No Third-Party Access

Moon Ritual does not collect, access, or store your intentions, journal entries, or personal data on any servers operated by Base11 Studios. Your data remains private to you at all times.

Analytics

Moon Ritual uses Mixpanel to collect anonymous usage analytics so we can understand which features are used and improve the app. This data is fully anonymized and never includes your intentions, journal entries, or any personally identifiable information. It is used only by Base11 Studios and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Notifications

If you turn on the message from the moon, Moon Ritual schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated and delivered on your device. No notification content is sent to or stored on any server operated by Base11 Studios.

Subscriptions

Moon Ritual offers an optional premium subscription handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchases are subject to Apple's privacy policy.
