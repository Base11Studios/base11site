Data Storage & Syncing

Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device for immediate access. This includes your intentions, check-ins, journal reflections, earned altar objects, and the moon you were born under.

iCloud Syncing (Optional)

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, Moon Keep uses Apple's iCloud to securely back up and sync your intentions, check-ins, and journal entries across all your Apple devices. This syncing:

  • Is completely private to you and stored in your personal iCloud account
  • Is not accessible by Moon Keep or Base11 Studios
  • Follows Apple's iCloud privacy and security standards
  • Can be disabled by signing out of iCloud on your device

No Third-Party Access

Moon Keep does not collect, access, or store your intentions, journal entries, or personal data on any servers operated by Base11 Studios. Your data remains private to you at all times.

Analytics

Moon Keep does not use any third-party analytics or tracking. We do not collect usage data, and nothing you write in the app is ever sold or shared with third parties.

Notifications

If you turn on the message from the moon, Moon Keep schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated and delivered on your device. No notification content is sent to or stored on any server operated by Base11 Studios.

Subscriptions

Moon Keep offers an optional premium subscription handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchases are subject to Apple's privacy policy.
