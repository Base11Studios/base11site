Data Storage & Syncing

Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device for immediate access.

iCloud Syncing (Optional)

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, Presmo uses Apple's iCloud to securely back up and sync your check-ins, journal entries, and activity history across all your Apple devices. This syncing:

  • Is completely private to you and stored in your personal iCloud account
  • Is not accessible by Presmo or Base11 Studios
  • Follows Apple's iCloud privacy and security standards
  • Can be disabled by signing out of iCloud on your device

No Third-Party Access

Presmo does not collect, access, or store your check-ins, journal entries, or personal data on any servers operated by Base11 Studios. Your data remains private to you at all times.

Analytics

Presmo uses Mixpanel to collect anonymous usage analytics so we can understand which features are used and improve the app. This data is fully anonymized and never includes your check-ins, journal entries, or any personally identifiable information. It is used only by Base11 Studios and is never sold or shared with third parties.
